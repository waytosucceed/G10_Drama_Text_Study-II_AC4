Sample Writing

Last year, my friend Meera told me she had a crush on a boy in our class. She made me swear not to tell anyone, and I swore.

A week later, during lunch, the others were sharing gossip, and I wanted something interesting to say. Before I could stop myself, her secret was out. Everyone gasped, and for one second I felt important.

That second did not last. By the end of the day, the whole class knew, and Meera knew exactly who had told. She didn’t shout or cry. She just said, “I trusted you,” and walked away. Those three words were worse than any argument.

I tried to apologise the next morning, but she moved her bag to another desk. It took two months before she spoke to me properly again.

I learned that a secret is not mine to spend. The moment of attention I gained was tiny compared with what I lost. Trust takes years to build and only one sentence to break, and I was the one who said it.

Writing Activity:

Have you ever betrayed the trust of a friend? If not, imagine that you have. Write about it.
